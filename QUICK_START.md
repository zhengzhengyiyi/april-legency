# Quick Start Guide - Mine Dimensions

## 🎯 Goal
Create custom mine dimensions, complete challenges, earn rewards!

---

## ⚡ 5-Minute Quick Start

### Step 1: Get the Mine Crafter
- Open creative inventory
- Go to **Functional** tab
- Find **Mine Crafter** block
- Place it in the world

### Step 2: Get Mine Ingredients
- Still in **Functional** tab
- Find **Mine Ingredient** items
- Take several different ones
- Each has different effects

### Step 3: Craft Your First Mine
1. Right-click the Mine Crafter
2. Put 1-3 ingredients in the slots
3. Click the craft button
4. A glowing portal spawns above!

### Step 4: Enter the Mine
1. Walk into the glowing beam
2. Wait 3 seconds
3. You're teleported to the mine!

### Step 5: Find the Exit
- Look for another glowing beam
- Or craft an **Eye of Exit** (Combat tab)
- Throw it to locate the exit
- Walk into the exit portal

### Step 6: Collect Rewards
- You're teleported back
- Rewards drop from the Mine Crafter
- Experience, keys, new ingredients!

## 🎮 Controls

- **Right-click Mine Crafter** → Open GUI
- **Right-click with Mine Item** → Activate mine
- **Right-click with Eye of Exit** → Throw to locate exit
- **Right-click door with Key** → Unlock secret area
- **Walk into portal** → Teleport

---

## 💡 Tips for Beginners

1. **Start Simple**: Use 1-2 ingredients for your first mine
2. **Bring Gear**: Food, weapons, torches
3. **Use Exit Eye**: Makes finding exits much easier
4. **Read Effects**: Check what each ingredient does
5. **Upgrade**: Donate XP to unlock more ingredient slots

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Portal won't spawn?**
- Make sure there's 3+ blocks of space above Mine Crafter

**Can't find exit?**
- Craft Eye of Exit from Combat tab
- Throw it to see direction

**Mine Crafter won't accept ingredients?**
- Upgrade it by donating 20 XP levels
- Click the DONATE button in GUI

**Lost in mine?**
- Use Eye of Exit
- Build a tall pillar to see farther
- Dying respawns you in overworld (but you fail the mine)
